Abandoned Souls

Psychological horror in a decaying world

About the Game

Abandoned Souls is a first-person psychological horror game that focuses on atmosphere, tension, and exploration rather than constant action. You wander through eerie, abandoned locations where every sound and shadow can make you question what is real.

The story follows a lone protagonist trying to uncover the truth behind a series of mysterious disappearances. As you progress, you piece together notes, recordings, and environmental clues that reveal a dark past and the fate of those who came before you.

The game emphasizes slow-burn horror, immersive sound design, and subtle visual details. There are no cheap jump scares every minute—fear comes from the feeling of being watched, from the unknown, and from the choices you make while exploring.

Key Features

  • Atmospheric exploration: Explore abandoned buildings, corridors, and hidden rooms.
  • Story-driven horror: Discover the truth through notes, clues, and environmental storytelling.
  • Psychological tension: Minimal HUD, no hand-holding, and a constant sense of unease.
  • Immersive audio: 3D sound and ambient noises that keep you on edge.
