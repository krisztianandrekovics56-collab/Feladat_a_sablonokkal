Fears to Fathom: Scratch Creek

Two friends, one terrifying night

About the Episode

Scratch Creek is a co-op horror episode in the Fears to Fathom series. The story follows Tessa and Marcus, two friends who experience a chilling event reminiscent of a previous Fears to Fathom case.

The two must work together to survive the night as strange occurrences escalate around their remote location. Communication and cooperation are essential — one mistake can cost both of them dearly.

The episode blends atmospheric horror with multiplayer tension, making every decision feel heavier when someone else’s safety depends on you.

Key Features

  • Online co-op: Play as Tessa or Marcus with a friend.
  • Shared tension: Both players experience events differently.
  • Dynamic storytelling: Choices and reactions shape the outcome.
  • Atmospheric horror: Subtle, slow-building fear instead of cheap scares.
